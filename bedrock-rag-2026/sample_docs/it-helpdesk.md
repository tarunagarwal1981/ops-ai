# IT helpdesk guide (sample document)

This is a made-up guide for testing the knowledge base. It does not describe any real company.

## Password resets
Office staff reset their own passwords on the self-service portal. Resets for onboard accounts go through the master, who raises a ticket with the IT helpdesk.

## Response times
Priority 1 tickets (vessel systems down): response within 1 hour, 24/7.
Priority 2 tickets: response within 1 business day.

## New laptops
Request new laptops through your line manager at least 10 business days before the start date.
