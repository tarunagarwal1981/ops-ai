# Crew change policy (sample document)

This is a made-up policy for testing the knowledge base. It does not describe any real company.

## Notice
Ratings: the crewing team needs 21 days' notice to plan a crew change.
Officers: the crewing team needs 30 days' notice, because relievers need a handover briefing.

## Who approves
The fleet manager approves every crew change. Changes in a port with no agent on file also need approval from the operations director.

## Travel
The company books all flights. Crew must not book their own tickets and claim them later.
