# Travel and expense policy (sample)

*Fictional policy, for testing only.*

## Flights

Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours. Business class needs written approval from a director.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is USD 180 in most cities and USD 260 in Singapore, London, Tokyo and New York.

## Claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the trip, with receipts for anything over USD 25.
